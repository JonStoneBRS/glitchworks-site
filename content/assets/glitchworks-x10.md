The X10 is a conducted energy weapon built for UK police roleplay.

Officers arm it, give a warning, and fire two probes that have to land properly to cause a lock-up. Every use is logged, so supervisors can review what happened.

Trainers get a dedicated range with a dummy, dye marks where probes land, and a scorecard at the end.

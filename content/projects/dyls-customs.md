A full vehicle workshop built for the London Metro server, with the scripts to match.

Drive onto the rolling road, strap the car down and do a full power run to unlock the tune menu. Cars on the lifts lock in place as they rise and are repaired at full height.

The site joins the workshop, an impound yard and a car lot, with motorised sliding gates, beacons and AI traffic kept out of the yard.

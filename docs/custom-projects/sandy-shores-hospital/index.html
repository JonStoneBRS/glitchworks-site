    Sandy Shores Hospital

    A full A&E hospital with a working helipad, built for Sandy Shores.

    Features

    • A&E with reception, majors, ward and staff room
    • 22 x 22 m helipad with a stretcher ramp and night lighting
    • Working doors, proper collision and a paved forecourt
    • Detailed interior with HQ textures and real clinical kit

    About

    A purpose-built NHS-style hospital on its own landscaped lot in Sandy Shores, made for the London Metro server.

    The helipad sits beside A&E, with a 1:12 stretcher ramp, handrails, a windsock and perimeter lights that come on at night. When a helicopter is coming in, the ring lights run a chase to guide the pilot down.

    Inside, every room is dressed with proper kit: beds, monitors, crash trolleys, counters with glass screens and a staff kitchen.

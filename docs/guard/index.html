    Safeguarding

    Helping your team look out for people

    Discord servers bring together people of all ages. Glitch Guard's optional safeguarding checks give your senior team a private heads-up when a message suggests someone could be at risk, so a real person can step in.

    Not AI

    A rules-based system using word and pattern lists, so it's predictable and transparent. Server admins choose which checks are on.

    Private alerts, never punishment

    It watches text messages and privately alerts only the senior roles your server picks, plus the owner, with a "Go to message" button. It never punishes anyone for these.

    Optional: Remove details

    With Remove details on, personal details, a child's age and doxxing are deleted straight away. Staff still get the alert.

    What it checks for

    • Self-harm or suicide riskStaff get UK support numbers to share.Samaritans 116 123Shout 85258Childline 0800 1111
    • Grooming-style messagesSecrecy, asking for pictures, moving the chat to other apps, or asking "are you alone".
    • Threats of violenceReal-world threats of violence.
    • Personal detailsPeople sharing UK mobile numbers, postcodes, street addresses or email addresses.
    • Children sharing their ageMessages such as "I'm 13" or "I'm in Year 8".
    • DoxxingPosting someone else's real name, address, number or school.

    Honest limits

    • Text only. It can't see images, GIFs, video or voice.
    • Pattern-based, so it can miss things or flag harmless messages.
    • A helping hand, not a replacement for a team.
    • Staff aren't checked unless Check staff is turned on (useful for testing).
    Protection

    Every protection option

    Choose Relaxed, Balanced or Strict, then switch any of these on or off.

    Anti-nukeStops mass deletes, bans and kicks.
    Permission guardUndoes admin permissions given by untrusted people.
    Bot guardRemoves bots added by untrusted people.
    Anti-raidLocks the server during join floods.
    Scam and phishing linksDeletes the message and bans the sender.
    Spam filterCatches message spam.
    Mass-mention filterCatches messages that mention 6 or more people.
    No links from brand-new membersBrand-new members can't post links.
    Block other servers' invitesRemoves invite links to other Discord servers.
    Guard network ban listKeeps out scammers and nukers already caught in other Glitch Guard servers.

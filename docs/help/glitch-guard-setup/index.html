    Guide

    Set up Glitch Guard

    A plain-English walkthrough: add the bot, give it the right role position, then run /setup and choose what suits your server.

    Add Glitch Guard to your server#

    Glitch Guard is free. To add it you need the Manage Server permission on the Discord server you want to protect.

    Add Glitch Guard to your server

    1. Press the button above and choose your server from the list.
    2. Discord shows the permissions Glitch Guard asks for. Leave them all ticked so every feature works.
    3. Press Authorise. Glitch Guard joins your server straight away.

    Move its role near the top#

    Discord only lets a bot manage roles and members that sit below its own role. If Glitch Guard's role is near the bottom, it can't give people the Verified role, time out or ban raiders, or undo permission changes.

    1. Open Server Settings > Roles.
    2. Find the Glitch Guard role and drag it up, above your member, Verified and staff roles. Just under your owner or admin roles is ideal.
    3. Save the changes.

    Glitch Guard can never act on the server owner, whatever its role position. That's a Discord rule.

    Run /setup#

    Type /setup in any channel and press Enter. A step-by-step wizard walks you through 10 steps.

    Every step has a recommended button that creates everything for you, so if you're not sure, pick the recommended option and move on. Nothing is final: you can change any step later with /settings.

    The 10 steps explained#

    Here's what each step does, what we recommend and what the choices mean.

    1Step 1: Staff role

    What it is
    The role your moderators have. People with it can see tickets and logs and use the moderation commands. Admins always can, whether or not they have the role.
    We recommend
    Pick your existing moderator role. If you don't have one yet, use the recommended button.
    Your choices
    Choose any role. Only give it to people you trust with tickets, logs and moderation.

    2Step 2: Roles and channels

    What it is
    A starter set of roles and channels to suit your type of server. It only adds things and never deletes anything.
    We recommend
    Use the starter set if your server is new or still being built. You can rename or move anything afterwards.
    Your choices
    Pick the server type closest to yours to get a matching starter set, or skip this step if you're happy with your current layout.

    3Step 3: Logs channel

    What it is
    A private channel that only staff can see. It records joins and leaves, deleted and edited messages, moderation actions, honeypot catches and ticket transcripts as PDFs.
    We recommend
    Turn it on. It's the easiest way to see what happened and when.
    Your choices
    Let Glitch Guard create the channel, or choose one you already have.

    4Step 4: Protection

    What it is
    The automatic defences. Pick a level, then switch individual options on or off.
    We recommend
    Balanced suits most servers.
    Your choices
    Relaxed is the lightest touch, Strict is the tightest, and Balanced sits in between. Each option is explained below.
    Protection options
    OptionWhat it does
    Anti-nukeStops mass deletes, bans and kicks.
    Permission guardUndoes admin permissions given by untrusted people.
    Bot guardRemoves bots added by untrusted people.
    Anti-raidLocks the server during join floods.
    Scam and phishing linksDeletes the message and bans the sender.
    Spam filterCatches message spam.
    Mass-mention filterCatches messages that mention 6 or more people.
    No links from brand-new membersBrand-new members can't post links.
    Block other servers' invitesRemoves invite links to other Discord servers.
    Guard network ban listKeeps out scammers and nukers already caught in other Glitch Guard servers.

    5Step 5: Verification

    What it is
    A #verify channel with a button. Pressing it gives the member the Verified role.
    We recommend
    Turn verification on. Lock the server too if you've had trouble with bots or raids.
    Your choices
    Optionally lock the server, so members who haven't verified can only see #verify.

    6Step 6: Honeypot

    What it is
    A #do-not-post channel. Real members are told to leave it alone, so anything that posts there is almost certainly a bot, and it gets caught. Catches show in your logs.
    We recommend
    Turn it on. It costs nothing and catches spam bots early.
    Your choices
    On or off.

    7Step 7: Tickets

    What it is
    A #support channel where members open private tickets. When a ticket closes, its transcript is saved as a PDF.
    We recommend
    Start with a preset. You can add more ticket types later with /settings.
    Your choices
    Pick presets or your own ticket types, and choose which teams can see each type.

    8Step 8: Welcome messages

    What it is
    A message that greets people when they join.
    We recommend
    Turn it on if you'd like new members to feel welcome. It's optional.
    Your choices
    On or off.

    9Step 9: Safeguarding (optional)

    What it is
    Rules-based checks that privately alert your senior team about worrying messages. It's not AI and it never punishes anyone. How safeguarding works.
    We recommend
    Pick a small group of trusted senior roles to receive the alerts, and turn on the checks that fit your community.
    Your choices
    Choose the senior roles that get alerts (the owner always gets them), choose which checks are on, and set two switches: Remove details deletes personal details, a child's age and doxxing straight away (staff still get the alert), and Check staff also checks staff messages, which is useful for testing.

    10Step 10: Partnership (optional)

    What it is
    Get your server listed in the Glitchworks Discord.
    We recommend
    Join if you'd like more people to find your server.
    Your choices
    Every Glitch Guard server gets a 5% store discount, and partners get 10%. Use /discount to get yours.

    Changing things later: /settings#

    Use /settings at any time to change any of the 10 steps: add a ticket type, change your protection level, pick different safeguarding roles, and so on. You don't need to run the whole setup again.

    Testing safeguarding#

    Staff aren't checked by default, so if you test from your own staff account, nothing happens. That's expected. To test, either:

    • turn on Check staff in /settings (and turn it off again when you're done), or
    • post from a non-staff account, such as a spare test account.

    Use harmless test messages. For example, to test the personal details check, use a made-up mobile number from the 07700 900000 to 07700 900999 range, which Ofcom keeps aside for TV and drama.

    Troubleshooting#

    Glitch Guard problems and fixes
    ProblemFix
    The bot can't manage roles, give the Verified role, time out or banIts role is too low. Open Server Settings > Roles and drag the Glitch Guard role above the roles and members it needs to manage. See how.
    Slash commands like /setup are missingWait a minute, then restart Discord (Ctrl+R on desktop). If they still don't show, add the bot again with the button at the top of this guide. You don't need to remove it first.
    Safeguarding test alerts don't arriveStaff aren't checked unless Check staff is on. Turn it on, or test from a non-staff account. More on testing.
    New members can't see any channelsThe server is locked for unverified members. They need to press the button in #verify. You can change this in Step 5 with /settings.

    How buying works#

    Buy through Tebex

    Tebex handles payment, tax and receipts for us. You'll log in with your FiveM account during checkout.

    Download from Keymaster

    Your purchase appears in the Cfx Keymaster straight away.

    Install

    Put the folder in your resources, add ensure lines to server.cfg in the order shown in the notes, then restart the server.

    Read the full install guide

    Questions#

    Which FiveM account should I use at checkout?

    Use the account that owns your server's license key. Assets are tied to that account and only run on servers using one of its keys.

    Where do I download what I bought?

    From the Cfx Keymaster, under your granted assets. You'll also get an email with a download link.

    Why can't I see or edit some of the code?

    Paid scripts are protected by Cfx's Asset Escrow so they can't be leaked. Config files are left open so you can set things up for your server.

    "You lack the required entitlement" error

    Your server key belongs to a different FiveM account from the one that bought the asset. Use a key from the buying account, or transfer the asset in Keymaster.

    Do you support QBCore, Qbox and ESX?

    Our scripts run standalone and include bridges for QBCore, Qbox and ESX. Each asset page lists what it works with.

    Can I get a refund?

    Contact us first and we'll do our best to fix any problem. Payments go through Tebex, so their checkout terms also apply.

    Can I share or resell what I buy?

    No. Everything is licensed for use on your own servers only.

    Glitch Guard questions#

    Is Glitch Guard free?

    Yes. Glitch Guard is free for any Discord server. Add it with the button on the Glitch Guard page, then run /setup.

    Does Glitch Guard's safeguarding use AI?

    No. It's a rules-based system using word and pattern lists, so it's predictable and transparent. Server admins choose which checks are on, and it only ever alerts staff. It never punishes anyone for these.

    How do I change a setting after setup?

    Use /settings. You can change any of the 10 setup steps at any time.

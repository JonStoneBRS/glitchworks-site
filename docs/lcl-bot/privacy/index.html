    LCL Bot

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated 4 November 2026

    This policy explains what information LCL Bot keeps when you use the London City Life Discord, why, and for how long. LCL Bot is built and run by Glitchworks for London City Life.

    What we keep

    • Your Discord user ID, username, server nickname, account age and roles.
    • Tickets you open: the form answers, messages and files in the ticket, and the transcript saved when it closes.
    • Moderation records: warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, strikes, staff notes and their reasons.
    • Messages that trip a filter, the spam-catching channel or the AI safety check, with the reason they were flagged.
    • Role requests, callsigns, event sign-ups and join check results.

    What we don't keep

    LCL Bot does not read or store your direct messages to other people. It does not keep a copy of ordinary chat messages that don't trip a filter. It does not sell data or use it for advertising.

    AI safety check

    To catch scams, threats and similar problems, messages that a basic filter has already marked as suspicious may be sent to an AI service for a second check. Only those messages are sent, never your DMs, tickets or staff channels. The AI never bans or kicks anyone; a member of staff decides.

    Why we keep it

    To run support tickets, keep the community safe, enforce the server rules, and keep a fair record of staff decisions.

    How long we keep it

    • Ticket transcripts: 12 months, then deleted.
    • Moderation records and staff notes: for as long as they are needed to enforce the rules, and reviewed at least once a year.
    • Flagged messages: 90 days, unless they form part of a moderation record.
    • Join check results: 90 days.

    Who can see it

    London City Life staff, depending on their role. Staff complaints are visible to management only. The bot runs on a hosting provider that stores the data on our behalf, and Discord itself handles all messages under its own privacy policy.

    Your rights

    You can ask for a copy of the information LCL Bot holds about you, ask for something to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Some moderation records may be kept where they are needed to enforce a ban. Open a ticket in the London City Life Discord to make a request.

    Changes

    We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

    Contact

    Privacy questions: open a ticket in the London City Life Discord, or contact Glitchworks through our Discord.

    LCL Bot

    Terms of Service

    Last updated 4 November 2026

    LCL Bot is the Discord bot for the London City Life community server. It is built and run by Glitchworks for London City Life. By using LCL Bot in the London City Life Discord, you agree to these terms.

    What LCL Bot does

    • Opens and manages support tickets, and saves ticket transcripts.
    • Handles role requests, callsigns and community events.
    • Helps staff keep the server safe: automatic moderation, checks on new members, a spam-catching channel and an AI safety check on suspicious messages.
    • Gives staff moderation tools such as warnings, timeouts, bans and message clean-up.

    Using it properly

    • Use the bot only as intended, within Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines and the London City Life rules.
    • Don't try to break, overload, trick or misuse the bot, its commands or its buttons.
    • Don't use tickets to harass staff or other members, or to send spam.

    Moderation

    Decisions about warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, roles and refunds are made by London City Life staff, not by the bot. The bot only carries out what staff decide, apart from removing clear spam and scam links automatically. Appeals go through a ban appeal ticket.

    Availability

    We aim to keep LCL Bot running, but it is provided as it is, with no promise that it will always be available or free of errors. Features may change or be removed.

    Ending use

    London City Life staff can stop anyone using the bot, for example after a ban. You can stop using it at any time by leaving the server.

    Changes

    We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use the bot after a change means you accept the new terms.

    Contact

    Questions about these terms: open a ticket in the London City Life Discord, or contact Glitchworks through our Discord.
